## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Unified style library
The plugin SHALL bundle the 86 2D and 37 3D styles under namespaced ids (`2d/<id>`, `3d/<id>`) and resolve a user's style choice to exactly one style before building.

#### Scenario: Ambiguous bare id
- **WHEN** the user names `low-poly`, which exists in both libraries
- **THEN** the skill asks whether they mean the 2D or the 3D style

#### Scenario: Style described, not named
- **WHEN** the user describes a look instead of naming a style
- **THEN** the skill suggests three styles from the catalog, points to the gallery page, and waits for a choice

### Requirement: Styled scenes render through HyperFrames
A styled scene SHALL be a HyperFrames composition whose canvas is driven from the scene timeline, rendered at the composition's size and aspect ratio, with its synthesized sound mixed as an audio stem.

#### Scenario: Mixed composition
- **WHEN** a film contains a UI scene, a 2D styled scene and a 3D styled scene
- **THEN** one `hyperframes render` produces the film with all three, and two renders are frame-identical

#### Scenario: Vertical channel
- **WHEN** the brief lists 9:16
- **THEN** styled scenes are re-laid out for 1080×1920, not cropped

### Requirement: Style gallery
The plugin SHALL ship a static gallery page with at least one still per bundled style, filterable by medium and family.

#### Scenario: Style added
- **WHEN** `add-style` imports a new style
- **THEN** its stills are rendered and it appears in the catalog and the gallery

### Requirement: Treatment and storyboard gates
The brief SHALL include a treatment with three candidate structures, and the storyboard SHALL default on and be skippable per brief.

#### Scenario: Storyboard on
- **WHEN** the brief is approved with the storyboard on
- **THEN** key frames are rendered to a contact sheet, inspected by Claude, shown, and the build waits for approval

#### Scenario: Storyboard off
- **WHEN** the brief records `storyboard: off`
- **THEN** the build starts after brief approval

### Requirement: Kokoro local TTS
The plugin SHALL use Kokoro as the default voice engine and ElevenLabs when the language is unsupported, a cloned voice is required, or the brief asks for premium delivery.

#### Scenario: Dutch voiceover
- **WHEN** the brief's voiceover language is Dutch
- **THEN** the brief records ElevenLabs and says why

#### Scenario: Model integrity
- **WHEN** the downloaded Kokoro model does not match its pinned checksum
- **THEN** the script stops with an error and generates nothing

#### Scenario: TTS round trip
- **WHEN** a TTS line is generated by either engine
- **THEN** it is transcribed with whisper and any mismatch with the script is reported

### Requirement: Readability check
The render check SHALL fail when any on-screen text or caption is held shorter than the reading rule.

#### Scenario: Short caption
- **WHEN** a six-word caption is on screen for 1.2 s
- **THEN** `readcheck` fails and names the text and its time

### Requirement: Fresh-context review
Before a render is shown as final, a reviewer with no build context SHALL review it against the rubric and report findings with frame numbers.

#### Scenario: Unresolved P0
- **WHEN** the reviewer reports a P0 finding
- **THEN** the render is not delivered until it is fixed

## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Standalone explainer plugins
**Reason**: `explainer` and `3d-explainer` are merged into `video-production`.
**Migration**: Install `video-production` 2.0.0. The same requests ("animated explainer", "3D explainer", Atlas style names) trigger `video-styles`.
