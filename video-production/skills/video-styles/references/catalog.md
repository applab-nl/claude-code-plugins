# Video style catalog

Pick a look by scanning the one-line entries; each id is namespaced as `<medium>/<style>` (2d, 3d, ui). A bare id such as `voxel` resolves when only one style has it; when it exists in several media (low-poly, neon-sign, voxel, x-ray), ask which one is meant. The gallery page shows stills once the plugin has been set up.


## 2D — Art & Design Movements

- **Art Deco Foil** (`2d/art-deco`) — Black lacquer and gold geometry, symmetrical stepped forms, sunburst rays. Good for: Premium launches, luxury-toned brand stories.. Motion: Stepped reveals, gilded title-sequence builds.
- **Bauhaus Poster** (`2d/bauhaus`) — Primary colors, bold geometric shapes and strict grid composition on a poster. Good for: Design-minded concepts, structured arguments, cultural topics.. Motion: Shapes snap into place along grid rules.
- **Constructivist Propaganda** (`2d/constructivist`) — Red, black and cream agitprop diagonals with heavy sans type. Good for: Manifestos, campaigns, bold calls to action.. Motion: Staccato slams, shapes rolling and cutting in.
- **Memphis Group** (`2d/memphis`) — Clashing pastel grounds with squiggles, confetti shapes and bold patterns. Good for: Playful lifestyle brands, youth campaigns, events.. Motion: Cut-out shapes hop on twos.
- **International Typographic Style** (`2d/swiss-grid`) — Strict modular grid, flush-left sans type, one red accent. Good for: Corporate clarity, design systems, rigorous information.. Motion: Rules draw along the grid, then type slides in.
- **Ukiyo-e Woodblock** (`2d/ukiyo-e`) — Edo-period woodblock print with Prussian blue and flat outlines. Good for: Japanese or nature themes, waves, dramatic scale.. Motion: Colors print in passes, waves roll across.
